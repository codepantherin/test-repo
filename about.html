About

Designer & Visual Storyteller

Five years of turning ideas into bold, minimal designs for brands that want to be remembered.

01

My Story

Hi, I'm Arsh Dhiman, a graphic designer with a keen eye for detail and a love for bold, minimal aesthetics. I work across branding, digital design, and visual storytelling, helping teams say more with less.

With over five years of experience, I've partnered with startups, agencies, and established brands to craft identities and interfaces that leave a lasting impression — from first sketch to final handoff.

02

Approach

  • Clarity first

    Every concept is stripped to its essentials until only the meaningful parts remain.

  • Systems, not one-offs

    Designs ship as reusable systems — tokens, grids, and components — so they scale.

  • Craft in the details

    Type, spacing, and motion are tuned until the work feels effortless to use.

03

Experience

Recent work spans brand identity for early-stage startups, product UI for SaaS teams, and print campaigns for cultural events. Each project starts with the same question: what is the one idea worth remembering?

Before going independent, I worked in studio environments where I learned to move fast without sacrificing polish — a habit I've carried into every collaboration since.
